About Me

This year, I decided to say yes to new challenges and to the future I want to build. I’m a Software Engineer in training, focused on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, DOM manipulation, responsive design, accessibility, and Git/GitHub. I’m learning how to think like a developer, breaking problems down, experimenting, and growing with each iteration. I serve as the Vice President of Student Government at Calbright College, where I support student initiatives and help create a community rooted in empathy and collaboration. Leadership has shaped the way I approach tech, reminding me that communication and curiosity are as essential as technical skills. I’m Brazilian, a lifelong learner, and a creative who loves watercolor and exploring the intersection between art and technology. That blend inspires the way I design and think about user experience. Technology, for me, is where imagination becomes something real. It’s a space to transform ideas into solutions that matter. As Eminem says, success is my only option, failure is not.
